> – Holland & modified Holland, CONCAWE and Briggs

> • Mostly used is the Briggs plume rise model
> – Gary Briggs, first published in 1965, updated 1969,1970,1972
> – Empirically derived based on observational data
> – Proposed for hot, bend-over plumes, less accurate for cold and
