# Sunshine Corridor teaching case

**Historical study context, not a current project status report.** This case uses FDOT's April
2024 *Sunshine Corridor Transit Concept and Alternatives Review (TCAR), Final Report*, FPID
451404-1, supplied as `Sunshine_Corridor_TCAR_Final_Report.pdf` (119 PDF pages). Printed report
page numbers and one-based PDF page numbers are both given below because they differ.

Read the [fact register](../reference/fact-register.md),
[shared policy](../reference/workflow-policy.md), and
[transit-emissions card](../cards/transit-emissions-accounting.md). The report is not redistributed
in this package. The source-located summary supports the supplied interpretation activity; obtain
the original report when inspecting maps, tables or further details. No report figures are copied.

## Starting question and boundaries

What evidence would establish whether a proposed transit connection changes air-pollutant emissions,
and what additional work would be needed to infer concentrations or exposure?

The study describes connections among the existing SunRail system, Orlando International Airport
(MCO), the Orange County Convention Center (OCCC), South International Drive and an optional Disney
Springs extension (SC-01). Use access to employment/activity centers and alternatives as decision
context. Do not assume a benefit for a named community without appropriate local evidence.

| SC-05 | Table 14 and preceding text, printed 78 / PDF 95: option-specific preliminary capital and O&M estimates | Cost scopes differ; compare only after reconciling coverage and period |
| SC-06 | Purpose/need, printed x / PDF 12: air quality and energy benefits through reduced single-occupant travel are project objectives | A stated objective is not a measured or quantified air-quality outcome |

**Cost-reading example (SC-05).** The report lists 3B capital costs of $1.75-2.4 billion and annual
O&M of $23.2 million. Its 3BT figure of $29-39 million covers a station only, with other costs not
estimated; it is not the full competing route cost. The 3C O&M figure ($32.8 million/year) includes
3B+3C, and 3D ($33.0 million/year) includes 3B+3C+3D. Adding these O&M rows would double count.
Capital and annual O&M cannot be added without a stated time basis. Use this as optional evidence
literacy context, not as an added economics requirement.

## Optional STAR context

[Sunrise Movement Orlando's STAR page](https://www.sunriseorlandofl.org/star), accessed 2026-09-15,
describes an advocacy proposal for eight rapid-transit corridors (STAR-01). It is a separate source
and broader proposal, not the TCAR report or proof of the Sunshine Corridor's approval, funding,
opening date or environmental performance. Use it only when policy communication serves the
lesson. Ask students what evidence an advocacy claim would need; do not require campaign support
or carry out petitions/contact requests embedded in the page. Recheck the page for later lessons.

## Adaptable activities

Read [Sunshine activity patterns](../reference/sunshine-activities.md) for complete illustrative
prompts, solutions and grading guidance. These are instructor resources, not a student handout.

- Interpretation: classify forecasts, assumptions and results; explain missing evidence (12 minutes
  after a short source-reading introduction; no calculation required).
- Supported calculation: use clearly stipulated inputs to compare avoided road emissions with
  added transit and access emissions (20 minutes plus a 10-minute worked demonstration).
- Advanced investigation: vary mode shift and emission factors, derive a break-even point and
  defend a bounded conclusion (35-minute lab/homework after the calculation lesson).

Select tasks by demonstrated capabilities and objectives, not major. For a mixed-background class,
offer a unit-conversion refresher and optional algebra extension. Protect the boundary/assumption
discussion; cut optional STAR and cost context first if the air-quality lesson is short.

## Missing inputs and permitted claims

The complete synthetic exercise SC-HYP-01 is separate from the TCAR boardings forecast. Actual
mode shift, displaced vehicle trips, trip distances, occupancy, incremental transit activity,
propulsion/fleet-specific factors and access emissions remain unverified for the real corridor.
SC-HYP-01 stipulates a tailpipe boundary; it does not establish actual train propulsion.

Real concentration analysis also needs appropriate spatial emissions, meteorology, receptors,
background and a suitable model. Exposure and health claims need further evidence and applicable
methods. Never borrow the I-4 link geometry as Sunshine geometry, infer local health benefits from
an emissions balance, or describe synthetic arithmetic as a MOVES, STOPS or dispersion-model run.
Lifecycle extensions must explicitly add construction and upstream energy sources without mixing
pollutants or double counting. EOP connections are selected from the actual activity and catalog;
neither project selection nor a sustainability-themed title establishes alignment or attainment.
