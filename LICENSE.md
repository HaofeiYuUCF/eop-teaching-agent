## Card attribution

Each topic card in `cards/` names its author and institution in its front matter. Contributors
retain credit for their cards. Adapting a card means keeping the original author's attribution
and adding your own.

## Course material

Topic cards in this repository were seeded from course material developed by Haofei Yu for
ENV 4120 (Air Pollution and Hazardous Waste Control), ENV 5128 (Smart Air Quality Monitoring and
Air Pollution Control), and ENV 6106 (Air Pollution Modeling) at the University of Central
Florida. Figures and third-party images from those courses are not redistributed here.
