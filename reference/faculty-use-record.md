# Optional Faculty Use and Revision Record

Use this brief record when an instructor wants to share feedback or revise a resource. Answer only
relevant questions in ordinary language. It is not a required intake form, a validated research
instrument, or evidence that classroom use has occurred. Keep identifiable student work and private
course information outside the public repository.

## Context and resources

- Course context, date, and contributor, if supplied; use a non-identifying description when needed.
- Actual use: preparation only, classroom use, or another stated context. Do not assume classroom use.
- Cards/scenario/plan and revisions used; record no scenario when applicable.
- Support actually used: AI assistance, discussion with colleagues, or other support, if known.

## What changed and why

- Original teaching purpose and what was adapted.
- Corrections, explanation changes, or changed assumptions, with source locations where applicable.
- Preparation effort or difficulties, only if reported; do not invent estimates.

## Observations and next revision

- What the instructor actually observed, in what context, and with what supporting evidence.
- Interpretations or proposed improvements, distinguished from observations.
- Specific card, scenario, plan, or assessment that needs revision and the reason.
- Revision made, remaining question, or no action needed; link a resulting revision when available.

Route resource changes to Card Builder and lesson-choice changes to Intake; use Reviewer for the
affected artifact. Feedback does not automatically verify a scientific claim or demonstrate learning.
Any use of these records in a research study requires that study's separately specified procedures.
